Founder’s Story

Preserving the Sacred Stillness of Your Family’s Beginning.

Hello, I am Solène Varma—founder, mother, and principal portrait artist at Atelier Solène. My journey into maternity and newborn portraiture began eight years ago after the birth of my own daughter, when I realized how quickly the scent of newborn skin and the weight of a sleeping baby against your chest become a memory.

Trained in classical medium-format film portraiture in Paris before establishing our sunlit Bengaluru loft in 2018, I founded Atelier Solène with a singular vision: to replace stiff, overly posed studio photography with an intimate, editorial sanctuary where mothers feel effortlessly radiant and newborns are held with reverence.

Artistry & Expertise

Our Approach to Maternity & Newborn Craft

We believe the finest portraits arise when technical mastery meets deep emotional empathy.

01 • Approach to Maternity Portraiture

Empowering, Sculptural & Effortless

Many expecting mothers tell us they feel camera-shy before arriving. Through directional window lighting, gentle micro-adjustments in posture, and flowing silk drapery from our 65-piece couture wardrobe, we guide every movement so you feel like an editorial muse.

  • Private styling fitting with French lace, raw silk & cashmere gowns.
  • Flattering chiaroscuro and soft high-key daylight setups.
  • Intimate partner and older sibling portraits woven naturally into the session.
Explore Maternity Collections
02 • Newborn Photography Expertise

Baby-Led Safety & Pure Simplicity

Your newborn’s comfort and safety govern every moment in our studio. We never force unnatural poses or place babies in fragile containers. Instead, we follow your baby’s natural womb-like curl, supported by trained hands and organic merino wraps.

  • Studio warmed to a soothing 27°C with gentle white-noise acoustics.
  • Only one newborn family scheduled per day for hospital-grade sanitization.
  • Dedicated nursing & changing suite with organic wipes and bottle warmers.

Our Daylight Sanctuary

A Calm Haven Designed for New Parents

Located in a quiet heritage tree-lined enclave on Lavelle Road, our 1,800 sq. ft. studio feels like a serene Parisian apartment rather than a commercial photo studio.

14-Foot Daylight Windows

Diffused through sheer Belgian linen curtains for luminous, flattering skin tones without harsh flash near your newborn’s eyes.

The Couture Wardrobe Suite

Curated sizes XS to XXL in breathable silks, cashmeres, and neutral menswear shirts, plus handmade baby knits.
