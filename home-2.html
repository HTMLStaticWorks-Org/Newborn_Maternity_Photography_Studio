Curatorial Statement

Beyond Trends: Creating Museum-Worthy Family Portraiture

In an era of thousands of fleeting phone snapshots, how often are mothers truly present inside the frame? At Atelier Solène, we approach family photography as fine art—stripping away artificial backdrops in favor of emotional truth, architectural light, and tactile linen textures.

“Thirty years from today, your child will not look for perfection—they will look to see how tenderly they were held.”

— Solène Varma, Creative Director
